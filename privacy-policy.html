Privacy Policy for GlucoGoal: Daily Walk & Habit

Effective Date: November 2026 (App v1.3.1)

Important Health & Wellness Disclaimer: GlucoGoal: Daily Walk & Habit is a general lifestyle, fitness, and personal habit-tracking application designed strictly for daily routines, meal checklists, walking logs, and personal self-monitoring. It is not a medical device and does not measure blood sugar directly, nor does it provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of diabetes or any medical condition. Any informational trend estimates displayed in the app are calculated solely from user-entered logs and are not a substitute for laboratory testing or clinical monitoring. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making changes to your diet, exercise routine, or medication.

1. Local-First Health, Wellness & Activity Data Storage

We respect your privacy. No account creation, email sign-in, or registration is required to use GlucoGoal: Daily Walk & Habit. All personal entries you log inside the app—including daily habit and meal checklists, walking durations and distances, body weight/waist check-ins, personal wellness journal notes, and optional self-entered glucose readings—are stored locally on your device using on-device storage and Android SharedPreferences. We do not operate backend servers that collect, access, view, sell, or store your personal health, fitness, or wellness records.

2. Physical Activity Sensor & Optional Foreground Location

During user-initiated live walk sessions, the app may use the device step sensor (android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) strictly on your device to count steps and estimate walking distance. If you explicitly enable the optional GPS toggle during an active walk session, the app uses foreground device location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) strictly on your device to calculate outdoor walking distance in real time. Neither physical activity data nor location data is ever accessed in the background or transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising, Consent & In-App Purchases)

GlucoGoal: Daily Walk & Habit uses official Google Play services that may process limited technical identifiers strictly as required to deliver ads, manage privacy consent, or verify purchases:

  • Google AdMob (Non-Personalized Ads Only) & UMP Consent: The free tier displays ads via Google AdMob configured strictly for Non-Personalized Ads (npa=1), ensuring no health-related profiling or behavioral ad targeting occurs. Users in supported regions (including the EEA and UK) can review or update their ad consent choices at any time inside the app's Settings tab.
  • Google Play Billing (Subscriptions & One-Time Upgrades): All optional Pro upgrades—including auto-renewing Pro subscriptions (glucogoal_pro: 1-Month, 3-Month, 6-Month, and 12-Month plans), subscription plan changes, the optional one-time Lifetime Pro unlock (pro_lifetime_unlock), and purchase restoration—are processed securely by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card, UPI, or billing details. Auto-renewing subscriptions renew automatically through Google Play unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period in your Google Play subscription settings.

4. Offline Backup, Data Deletion & User Control

Because your personal logs remain strictly on your device, you maintain full control over your data at all times. You can export an offline local JSON backup file, reset all stored entries from the app's Settings tab, or permanently delete all local data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

GlucoGoal: Daily Walk & Habit is intended for general adult wellness, walking, and habit tracking and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because personal logs remain locally on the device and ads are strictly non-personalized, no personal profile data is collected by our servers.

6. Policy Updates & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or Google Play policy requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
