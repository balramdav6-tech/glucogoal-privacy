Privacy Policy for GlucoGoal: Habit & Walk Log

Effective Date: November 2026

Important Health & Wellness Disclaimer: GlucoGoal is a personal habit, meal-routine, and walking distance tracker designed strictly for general wellness and self-monitoring. It is not a medical device and does not measure blood sugar directly, nor does it diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent diabetes or any medical condition. Always consult a qualified physician before changing any medication or diet.

1. Local-First Health & Activity Data Storage

We respect your privacy. All personal entries you log inside GlucoGoal—including daily meal checklists, walking distances, body weight/waist notes, and self-entered glucose readings—are stored locally on your device using local storage and Android SharedPreferences. We do not operate backend servers that collect, view, sell, or store your personal health or wellness records.

2. Physical Activity Sensor & Optional Foreground Location

During user-initiated live walk sessions, the app uses the device step sensor (android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) solely on your device to count steps and estimate walking distance. If you explicitly enable the optional GPS toggle during a live walk session, the app uses foreground device location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) solely on your device to calculate outdoor walking distance in real time. Neither physical activity data nor location data is ever tracked in the background or transmitted to any external server.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising & In-App Purchases)

The free version of GlucoGoal uses official Google Play services that may process limited technical identifiers:

  • Google AdMob (Non-Personalized Ads Only): Configured strictly with Non-Personalized Ads (npa=1) so no health-related profiling or behavioral ad targeting occurs. AdMob may collect device identifiers and basic diagnostics for fraud prevention and ad delivery.
  • Google Play Billing: Used securely by Google Play to process optional Pro subscriptions (glucogoal_pro), one-time Pro upgrades (pro_lifetime_unlock), and purchase restoration.

4. Data Deletion & Control

Because your personal logs remain on your device, you can export a backup file, reset all app data inside the app's Settings tab, or permanently delete all data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling the application.
