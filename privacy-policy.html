Privacy Policy

App Name: GlucoGoal: Habit & Walk Log (com.glucogoal.wellnesstracker)
Effective Date: November 2026

Health & Wellness Disclaimer: GlucoGoal is a personal habit, meal-routine, and walking distance tracker designed for general wellness and self-monitoring only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

1. Personal Wellness & Activity Logs (100% Local)

All meal logs, water intake, custom reminders, blood sugar self-monitoring entries, and walking distance records are stored locally on your device. We do not operate cloud servers and do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal health or wellness entries.

2. Optional Foreground Location (GPS)

If you grant optional foreground location permission during an active post-meal walk timer session, GPS coordinates are used strictly on your device in real time to calculate walking distance. Location data is never tracked in the background and never transmitted to external servers.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of GlucoGoal displays non-personalized advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and basic ad interaction/diagnostic telemetry in encrypted form to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. Purchasing the one-time GlucoGoal Lifetime Pro upgrade permanently disables ad requests.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your wellness logs reside solely on your phone, you have full control over them. You can export a local JSON backup, reset your logs inside the app, or permanently delete all local app data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
